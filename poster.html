What the 100 was hiding

  1. One score, many roads. The same full mark is reached in very different ways: windows crowded with students and windows where a few submit again and again; problems nearly solved on the first try and problems still short after three.
  2. Retries progress unevenly. % of eligible retries set a new best. The rate differs by homework, by how soon a retry follows the last attempt, and by where in the window it falls.
  3. Timing travels with the exam. In supplied grades, a lower final-day share went with higher midterm scores (ρ ). An association, not a cause.

Read with care

  • Observational data: associations, not causes or predictions.
  • Submissions are not effort. Time between submissions is not study time.
  • Windows and groups with fewer than five students are withheld, never shown as zero. Only aggregates leave the private data.
  • The exam sample is of students, with pre-exam homework only.
